Frequently Asked Questions

Which CAD formats are supported?

Currently: STEP / STP (.step, .stp, .p21), IGES / IGS (.iges, .igs), and DXF (.dxf). Native CAD formats (CATIA, NX, SolidWorks) are not currently supported.

Does AI override engineering calculations?

No. Deterministic engineering calculations are authoritative. AI assistance is advisory-only and always visually distinguished from validated engineering results.

Which machining processes are supported?

Turning, milling, drilling, threading, hole finishing, honing, and lapping — each with deterministic formulas for speed, feed, depth, MRR, power, torque, and time calculations.

Are engineering calculations traceable?

Yes. Every calculated result includes provenance: source rule, version, input values, and engineering reference — providing a complete audit trail.

Can company-specific tools and machines be added?

The catalog architecture supports registering custom tools, machines, and materials. This capability is planned for the Professional and Enterprise tiers.

Is CAM integration planned?

Advanced CAM integration is on the roadmap. Current focus is on the deterministic engineering core and CAD interoperability pipeline.
